I Am a Product of Grace

A testimony of grace, resilience and a life transformed by faith.

By Pastor Thenji Mthethwa30 August 2026

I am a product of

Grace

Pastor Thenji Mthethwa

Before my beauty industry days, the seeds of

entrepreneurship had already been sown in my life—though I did not yet have the language to define it. I was raised by a resilient single mother who bore the responsibility of providing for four daughters. Through her work of making jerseys, she sustained our household with dignity and determination. As the firstborn, I was introduced to responsibility at an early age. By the age of sixteen, I was actively assisting her, gaining not only practical skills but also invaluable lessons in sacrifice, perseverance, and commitment.

This formative season instilled in me a deep sense of responsibility long before it cultivated ambition. I came to understand that provision requires discipline, and that even modest contributions can have significant impact. Although the jersey business was seasonal, it embedded within me a mindset of resilience and resourcefulness. In retrospect, I recognize that this was a period of divine preparation—one in which I was being shaped through

FROM LEARNER TO LEADER IN THE BEAUTY INDUSTRY

diligence and compassion, even before I fully understood my purpose.

A pivotal moment came years later through what appeared to be a simple opportunity. I was invited by a family acquaintance to assist at a salon on weekends. There were no assurances of permanence or progression—only the opportunity itself. I accepted without hesitation. At the time, I was employed and still navigating my academic journey and uncertain about my future, yet I committed myself fully.

Through consistency, discipline, and a strong work ethic, I transitioned from a weekend assistant to a valued member of staff. It was within this professional environment that I encounter salvation. A colleague, Nokuthula Ndlovu, introduced me to a community of faith and consistently shared the message of Christ with me. The salon became more than a place of work; it became a space of spiritual growth. Through the Word of God, my perspective shifted significantly. I began to understand that human potential extends beyond survival—we are called to build, to multiply, and to lead with intention. My craft became more than a skill; it became a vehicle for purpose.

As my expertise developed, I encountered accomplished women who had established successful enterprises in various sectors, including retail and healthcare. Their journeys inspired me and broadened my perspective on what was possible. I realized that economic empowerment often requires the courage to pursue entrepreneurship. With this understanding, I began to prepare intentionally.

While maintaining my responsibilities, I invested gradually in tools and equipment, acquiring resources incrementally. Through patience and strategic planning, I laid the foundation for what would eventually become my own salon.

In due course, preparation met opportunity. I established my own business and transitioned into entrepreneurship. Beyond personal achievement, this milestone enabled me to create employment opportunities for others, extending the same opportunity that had once been afforded to me. However, my journey was not without profound adversity. In March 2024, my family experienced the devastating loss of my 25-year-old son. This tragedy profoundly affected every aspect of my life. The weight of grief was overwhelming, and in that season, I lost not only my emotional stability but also the business I had worked so diligently to build. It was not a failure of capability, but a consequence of deep personal loss.>
