From Corporate Comfort to Kingdom Assignment

A journey from corporate life toward a faith-led assignment and a new sense of purpose.

By Gail Manamela5 August 2026

From CORPORATE COMFORT to KINGDOM Assignment There are seasons when God disrupts

our comfort—not to harm us, but to reposition us. My journey into real estate was not a strategic career move or a reaction to corporate dissatisfaction. It was a divine instruction that demanded faith, surrender, and obedience.

ministry flowed through professionalism. Opportunities to mentor others, particularly in disadvantaged communities, revealed how real estate can transform lives—through skills development, employment, and generational wealth. Ministry in the marketplace is about compassion, faith, and purpose meeting practical action.

My Journey into Marketplace Ministry Through Real Estate

At the time, I had a stable corporate job. On paper, everything looked successful—but God was calling me to ministry beyond church walls. transactional, seemed unlikely: Real estate regulated, competitive. Yet God was expanding my understanding of ministry.

Today, I stand as a witness that obedience is never a loss—it is an investment. God has turned property into a platform for impact, provision, and restoration. And this journey? It’s only the beginning. WWM

Leaving faith. Commissions were uncertain, results demanded persistence, and there was no roadmap—only assurance that obedience would lead to provision. I soon realised: I was not merely changing careers; I was stepping into a Kingdom assignment.

Early encounters confirmed this calling. One client, blind, required guidance through a property contract. I prayed in the middle of the transaction—and her sight was restored. Another single mother, facing home repossession, received divine provision through a client’s extraordinary offer. Transactions became testimonies; properties became places of encounter. The marketplace had become holy ground.
