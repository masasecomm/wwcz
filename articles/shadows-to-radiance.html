From Shadows to Radiance

A story of overcoming hardship, abuse and painful beginnings to find renewed hope.

By Rox-Anne Maistry25 September 2026

From

Shadows toRadiance

By Rox-Anne Maistry

“I was told I was unworthy, but I refused to let my circumstances define me.”

R

ox-Anne Maistry’s story is a testament to the human spirit’s ability to overcome even the most daunting challenges. From a childhood marked by foster care, bullying, and abuse, to a life filled with pain, rejection, and disappointment, Rox-Anne’s journey is one of resilience, redemption, and ultimately, triumph. As she recounts her experiences, Rox- Anne shares the pain of being told she was unworthy, that she couldn’t shine, and that she was so messed up that God didn’t love her. But she also reveals the power of her own strength, as she refused to let her circumstances define her.

Rox-Anne’s path was not without its struggles. She faced the trauma of being sexually abused by her father, and the pain of three miscarriages. But through it all, she found the strength to keep going, to rise above her circumstances, and to use her experiences to help others. Today, Rox-Anne is a wife, mother of two daughters, an ordained pastor, and a motivational speaker.

She has written a book, “Who Told You?”, which will be released in August 2024. Her story is a reminder that even in the darkest of

times, there is always hope for a brighter future.

As Rox-Anne shares her story, she offers words of encouragement and advice to those who have experienced similar losses.

“Don’t ignore the pain,” she says. “Confront it and take the time to process your emotions. It’s okay to feel sad, angry, and hurt. But don’t let those feelings define you. Instead, use them as fuel to rise above your circumstances and find your purpose.”

Rox-Anne’s story is a beacon of hope for anyone who has ever felt like they were unworthy or that their pain was too much to bear. It is a reminder that no matter what life throws at us, we have the power to choose how we respond. And with courage, resilience, and faith, we can overcome even the most daunting challenges and emerge stronger, wiser, and more radiant than ever before. WWM
