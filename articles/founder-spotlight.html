Founder Spotlight: Faith, Creativity & Bold Leadership

A founder spotlight celebrating faith, creative vision and bold leadership.

By Joy Eunice16 June 2026

Today, Macrain represents thousands of actors, models, and creatives of all ages and backgrounds, providing opportunities locally, across Africa, and globally. The company has grown into a multifaceted hub, including MacCasting, MacProduction, and MacGlam, all operating with Christian values, prayer, and a heart for community.

Joy Eunice, founder of Macrain Holdings,

is proof that faith and creativity can turn a vision into a thriving empire. A born-again Christian, wife, and mother of two, Joy’s journey is one of resilience, bold ideas, and

God-led timing.

For Joy, talent isn’t just a skill—it’s potential waiting to be discovered, whether in a studio or on the street. Through Macrain, she empowers others to pursue their dreams, support their families, and thrive in their calling.

“I see opportunity everywhere,” she says. “With God’s guidance, creativity, and courage, anything is possible.” WWM

Connect with Joy at joy@macrain.co.za to explore opportunities in the creative industry.

Between 2008 and 2010, she hustled through small businesses and temporary gigs, searching for something meaningful. God’s plan revealed itself: a calling to the Film, TV, and Entertainment Industry. Early presenting gigs were fun but never felt like a career—until she noticed a gap in the industry for diverse, African talent.

Life tested her faith in 2012 when she battled severe pregnancy complications, Hyperemesis Gravidarum, which nearly cost her life. Surrounded by prayer, family support, and divine grace, she gave birth to a healthy daughter, Neriah. That season, though difficult, prepared her for the leap she would take the following year.

In 2013, Joy officially launched Macrain as a talent agency, starting with just herself as the first talent. With no formal media education, no office, and no capital, she relied on faith, creativity, and persistence. “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me” became her guiding principle.>
